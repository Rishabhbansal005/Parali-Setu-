## 1. Unit Economics & Pricing Model (Per Tonne Basis)

The financial model of ParaliSetu is anchored in real biomass gate pricing across Punjab and Haryana bio-CNG and industrial boiler plants.

### Price Breakdown per Delivered Tonne (Delivered at Plant Gate within 25 km)
```
Average Landed Plant Gate Purchase Price (Gross):           ₹2,600.00 / Tonne (100.0%)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
(-) Direct Field Baling & Raking Operations (Baler Owner):    ₹1,000.00 / Tonne  (38.5%)
(-) Short-Haul Logistics & Transport (Tractor-Trolley Crew):   ₹550.00 / Tonne  (21.2%)
(-) Farmer Stubble Payment / Incentive (Escrow Payout):        ₹650.00 / Tonne  (25.0%)
(-) Dharamkanta Weighment & Moisture Testing Fee:              ₹50.00 / Tonne   (1.9%)
(-) ParaliSetu Digital Platform Aggregation Fee (Commission):  ₹150.00 / Tonne   (5.8%)
(-) Working Capital Buffer / Contingency:                      ₹200.00 / Tonne   (7.7%)
```

### Key Highlights of the Unit Model:
1.  **Farmer Receives Clear Value:** On average, 1 acre yields 2.0 tonnes of recoverable baled stubble. The farmer receives **₹1,300 per acre in net cash**, while avoiding ₹2,500 in diesel for manual mulching or ₹5,000–₹15,000 in CAQM fines.
2.  **Baler Operator Earns High Margin:** ₹1,000/tonne baling fee yields ₹500/tonne net margin after diesel, twine, and driver costs, earning a baler owner ₹1,50,000–₹2,50,000 in pure profit during the 20-day peak season.
3.  **Sustainable Platform Take-Rate:** ParaliSetu captures **₹150 per tonne (5.8% of gross volume)** directly from the corporate buyer's procurement payment, completely free for the farmer.

## 4. Break-Even Analysis & Working Capital

1.  **Break-Even Tonnage:**
    *   Annual Fixed Overheads (Year 1): **₹30.0 Lakhs** ($36,000 USD).
    *   Gross Platform Contribution per Tonne: **₹150 / Tonne**.
    *   **Break-Even Volume:**
        $$\text{Break-Even Tonnes} = \frac{₹30,00,000}{₹150/\text{Tonne}} = \mathbf{20,000 \text{ Tonnes}}$$
    *   At typical yield of 2.0 tonnes/acre, ParaliSetu achieves complete cash break-even by aggregating just **10,000 acres** of farmland in a single district (less than 2.5% of Sangrur's paddy area).
2.  **Working Capital Mechanics (T+1 Cycle):**
    *   Bio-CNG plants deposit estimated procurement amounts into the ParaliSetu banking escrow account upfront before the harvest week begins.
    *   Upon weighment at the Dharamkanta, the gross/tare slip triggers an automatic ACH/UPI payout to the farmer and truck transporter within 24 hours.
    *   Because buyer capital is pre-funded into escrow, **ParaliSetu carries zero inventory risk and zero bad-debt receivables**.

## 5. Cost-Effectiveness Metrics (Impact ROI)

*   **Cost per Beneficiary Farmer:**
    *   Year 1 Total OPEX = ₹33.5 Lakhs across 12,500 participating farmers.
    *   **Platform Cost per Smallholder = ₹268 ($3.20 USD)**.
    *   Economic return generated per farmer = ₹2,600 (Net cash payout + saved mulch diesel).
    *   **Economic Return on Investment: ~9.7x**.
*   **Cost per Tonne of $\text{CO}_2$ Equivalent Abated:**
    *   Year 1 Net Operating Cost = ₹33.5 Lakhs.
    *   Emissions abated = 30,000 $\text{tCO}_2\text{e}$.
    *   **Abatement Cost = ₹111 ($1.33 USD) per tonne of $\text{CO}_2$ abated**, making ParaliSetu one of the lowest-cost climate mitigation interventions globally compared to carbon capture technologies ($50–$150/tCO₂e).

## 6. Sensitivity Analysis

| Stress Scenario Tested | Underlying Assumption Change | Impact on Year 2 Net Profit | Strategic Mitigation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Severe Early Rains in October** | Harvest delayed; baling window compressed to 10 days; 30% volume reduction. | Profit drops from ₹2.77 Cr to ₹1.65 Cr (Still profitable). | Multi-district geographic hedge (Punjab has dry days while Haryana gets rains; dynamic machinery shifting). |
| **Buyer Price Squeeze** | Biomass gate rate drops from ₹2,600 to ₹2,200/tonne due to cheap coal. | Platform fee adjusted from ₹150 to ₹120/tonne; Profit drops to ₹1.95 Cr. | Long-term off-take SLAs linked to SATAT mandatory CBG blending floor price. |
| **High Farmer Voice App Attrition** | Elderly farmers refuse mobile app; need in-person village agents. | Field staff expenses double from ₹42L to ₹84L. | Existing Dharamkanta weighbridge operators and Custom Hiring Centre staff handle manual walk-in bookings. |
