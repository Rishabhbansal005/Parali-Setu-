8. Key Partners

Industrial Offtakers:

  • Bio-CNG plants (under SATAT scheme, e.g. Verbio Sangrur).
  • 2G Bio-Ethanol plants (IOCL Panipat) & biomass pellet boilers.

Equipment Pools:

  • Custom Hiring Centres (CHCs) with subsidized balers/rakes.
  • Local tractor-trolley fleet operators.

Trust & Verification:

  • Village Dharamkanta (licensed independent weighbridges).
  • KVKs & Punjab/Haryana State Agriculture Departments.
6. Key Activities
  • PostGIS Clustering: Grouping 10–15 smallholdings into contiguous 35-acre runs.
  • Vernacular Voice AI: Processing Punjabi/Hindi speech to structured booking data.
  • Weighbridge Integration: Gross/tare ticket logging & instant escrow release.
7. Key Resources
  • Proprietary IP: PostGIS spatial routing engine & Indic voice intake model.
  • Agronomic Formula: PAU-verified yield lookup (2.0 tonnes/acre baler recovery).
  • Network: 500+ baler/tractor operators & SATAT buyers.
2. Value Propositions

For Smallholder Farmers:

  • Free field clearance within 48-72h of harvest.
  • ₹1,200–₹1,800/acre net income (₹650/tonne).
  • Zero CAQM fines + in-app No-Burn Certificate.

For Baler Operators:

  • Contiguous 30-acre runs eliminate deadhead transit miles.
  • +32% diesel fuel efficiency and higher seasonal earnings.

For Industrial Buyers:

  • Predictable, moisture-tested daily feedstock schedule.
  • Prevents expensive digester shutdowns.
4. Customer Relationships
  • Voice-First Simplicity: Zero learning curve for illiterate farmers.
  • Dharamkanta Neutral Scale: Weight verified locally, not at distant factory.
  • SMS/WhatsApp Receipts: Instant confirmation for non-smartphone users.
3. Channels
  • Flutter Mobile App: 17MB lightweight Android app with offline speech.
  • Next.js Operations Portal: Weighbridge gross/tare logging & buyer portal.
  • Local Nodes: Village Panchayats, CHCs, and Dharamkanta kiosks.
1. Customer Segments

Primary Beneficiaries:

  • Small & marginal farmers (1–4 acres) facing 15-day wheat sowing deadline. Pays ₹0.

Paying Customers:

  • Bio-CNG (CBG) refineries under the SATAT mandatory blending mandate (1%–5%).
  • Biomass pellet manufacturers & industrial thermal power boilers.

Supply Partners:

  • Baler and tractor-trolley equipment owners seeking peak seasonal utilization.
9. Cost Structure

Technology & Platform (Fixed):

  • Supabase PostgreSQL/PostGIS database & cloud hosting: ₹35,000/mo.
  • Voice Whisper/Gemini ASR tokens & transactional SMS: ₹0.27/intake.
  • Sentinel-2 satellite NBR verification processing overhead.

Field Operations (Peak Season Variable):

  • Village Cluster Coordinators (Kisan Mitras) across 15 pilot clusters.
  • Handheld digital pin probe moisture meters (₹2,500/unit).
  • Banking escrow compliance & third-party weighment auditing.
5. Revenue Streams & Commercial Model
  • B2B Offtake Take-Rate (Primary): ₹150 / Tonne (5.8% commission on ₹2,600 delivered gate rate paid by industrial buyers; 0% fee on farmers).
  • Baler Dispatch Optimization Fee: ₹40 / Tonne software fee for pre-clustered contiguous runs.
  • Enterprise Data & ESG Audit SaaS: Traceability certification for OMCs (IOCL, BPCL, HPCL).
  • Break-Even Volume: 20,000 Tonnes (~10,000 acres in 1 district). Year 3 Target: ₹15.0 Cr Net Revenue.
