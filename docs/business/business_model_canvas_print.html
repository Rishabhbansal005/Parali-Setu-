Value Propositions
For Smallholder Farmers (1–4 Acres)
  • Free Field Clearance: Stubble cleared in 48–72h before wheat sowing window.
  • Guaranteed Cash Earnings: ₹1,200–₹1,800/acre net income (₹650/tonne direct payout).
  • Zero Pollution Penalties: Avoid ₹5,000–₹15,000 CAQM fines + verified No-Burn Certificate.
For Baler & Fleet Operators
  • Contiguous 30–50 Acre Runs: Eliminates road transit diesel wastage ("deadhead miles").
  • +32% Machinery Utilization: Maximizes revenue during the critical 20-day peak.
For Industrial Biomass Offtakers
  • Predictable Daily Feedstock: Eliminates multi-crore boiler shutdowns.
  • Moisture-Tested Quality: Pre-screened strictly below 18% moisture.
